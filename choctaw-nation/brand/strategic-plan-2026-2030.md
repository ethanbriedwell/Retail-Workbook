# 2026–2030 Choctaw Nation Strategic Alignment

Source: [`source/strategic-plan-2026-2030.pdf`](source/strategic-plan-2026-2030.pdf).
At the center of the framework: **Faith · Family · Culture**, surrounded by *Opportunities for Growth and Prosperity*.

Tag decks, KPIs and initiatives with a pillar and objective so the work traces back to the plan.
The same list is exported as `strategicPillars` in [`../tokens/brand.ts`](../tokens/brand.ts).

## Culture and Sovereignty
We will assert, defend, protect, and promote our rights as a sovereign nation to improve the economic, environmental, social, and cultural prosperity of our people.
- Cultural Heritage and Language
- Environmental Sustainability and Protection
- Public Safety and Risk Management
- Self-Governance

## Empowering People
We will enhance the well-being of stakeholders by advocating, allocating resources, and remaining committed to improving quality of life, self-sufficiency, and independence.
- Communication and Accountability
- Learning and Development
- Service Coordination
- Health Outcomes
- Quality Education
- Sustainable Housing

## Best in Class
We will elevate our service delivery and competitive advantage using best practices through continuous improvement, exceptional customer service, and innovation.
- Operational Efficiency
- Service Excellence
- Technology Advancement
- Data-Driven Decisions

## Financial Stewardship
We will ensure financial sustainability by growing our revenue, reducing costs, and strengthening local economies.
- Revenue Growth
- Cost Optimization
- Economic Growth
- Strategic Growth
